# Code of Conduct

Do They Pass contributors should be able to discuss difficult engineering, privacy, safety, and identity-resolution problems without turning the project into a hostile place.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas, code, and assumptions rather than people.
- Treat privacy and safety concerns as legitimate engineering concerns.
- Do not post unnecessary personal information about real people.
- Use synthetic examples whenever possible.
- Respect contributor experience levels and ask clarifying technical questions in good faith.
- Keep disagreements focused on evidence, tradeoffs, and product impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory abuse
- doxxing or publishing private personal information
- using project discussions to investigate or target an individual
- sexualized harassment or unwanted sexual attention
- repeated bad-faith disruption
- encouraging misuse of the product for stalking, blackmail, harassment, discrimination, or regulated screening

## Real-person examples

Public issue threads are not a place to build dossiers on people. If a bug can only be reproduced with authorized real-person data, minimize the information shared and move sensitive details to an appropriate private channel.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, search subjects, or the project.

For conduct concerns that should not be handled publicly, use the contact options at https://dotheypass.com/contact.html.
